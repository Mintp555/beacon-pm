Core service areas
01
CARF Readiness Review

An honest look at where you stand against the CARF standards. I find the gaps, risks and priorities before you commit to the full implementation.

  • A conversation with leadership about your goals
  • Gap assessment against CARF standards
  • Risks, ranked by what matters most
  • A plan and timeline for implementation
  • Recommendations on who owns what
02
Project Management for CARF Implementation

I manage the implementation from start to finish. I work with each department, keep track of the documents and give leadership clear, honest updates on how close you are to being ready.

  • Implementation plan and milestones
  • Who owns what in each department
  • Document tracking and version control
  • Measures so you can see what's working and what to improve next
  • Raising risks and issues early
  • Keeping leadership informed and involved
Working approach
Quality that is real, and lasts long after the survey.
  • A clear plan with milestones and named owners
  • Weekly meetings, follow-ups and reviews
  • Document tracking and progress reports
  • Raising risks early, before they become problems
  • Measuring quality so you know what's already good and where to improve
